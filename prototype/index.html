Coach
Try these (each answers an [OPEN] question):
  • Beat grid: play a 3-letter word, then a 5-6 letter word, in both grid modes. A = cadence stretches with word length; B = fixed 4-beat volley, characters cram (gap shrinks). Which feels like "the enemy's rhythm"?
  • Space: wait for a two-word spell ("sad lad"). Type-space makes the gap a beat you play; auto-insert skips it. Does the channel help or interrupt flow?
  • Penalty: switch to strict mid-run — one wrong key kills the whole incantation. Is this motivating or rage-quit for a learner?
  • Cast feel: watch the word-bolt hit the archer — does the element color + impact sell "you cast a spell"? Waves tighten +4 bpm each; is the fight loop compelling?
  • Lanes: can you read the finger from color+position before you type it? The element + finger labels are under the hit line.
  • Audio delay: if the click and your keystroke feel offset, slide until they fuse.
