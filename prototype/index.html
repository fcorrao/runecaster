How a run goes: chapters of short encounters — archers (steady volleys), cavalry (the charge speeds up each spell), and a boss (its incantations follow a rhythm pattern, off-beat runes included). Clear a chapter and the incantations grow: short two-word spells → longer derived words → three-word invocations → numbers → symbols. Spells are phrases in one invented tongue (the gloss under the word says what you're casting), and each encounter keeps to one school of words, so they repeat and become learnable.
  • Penalty: switch to strict mid-run — one wrong key kills the whole incantation. Is this motivating or rage-quit for a learner?
  • Pacing: are encounters short enough early on, and does each chapter feel like a step up? "start at" jumps straight to a later chapter.
  • Sigils (thumb): defeating an enemy earns its school's sigil (bosses give 2); three flawless spells earn one too. Press Space and time slows to a crawl (the same slow speed at any tempo; letters are hidden so it can't be used to read ahead), the panel between your hands opens into a spellbook showing what every sigil does (new ones are tagged NEW until you've read them) — then type the sigil's name — wynkai Gale (push the runes back a bar), dunfen Bulwark (block the next strike), moxquo Veil (next 6 runes take any key of the right finger), gamsel Stillwater (next 2 spells 20% slower), vorlum Kindle (next spell at double power). A wrong letter fizzles (charge kept); Space again cancels; the ring can open any time, and closes itself only when a rune reaches the line (you still have the late half of its window to hit it). The runes keep creeping while you cast — that distance is the price.
  • Lanes: can you read the finger from color+position before you type it? The element + finger labels are under the hit line.
  • When to press: the letter crossing the white line — the tile filling its outlined slot. The two thin lines around the hit line are the PERFECT window (±55 ms); the tile touching the line at all is the GOOD window (±140 ms). Every judgment shows its error in ms, and the bar at the top of the battle stage plots your last 20 presses.
  • Calibration: if the click doesn't land when the letter crosses the line, slide audio. If your presses sit consistently early/late on the error bar, press "set input from last 20 hits" (or slide input). Both are saved in this browser.
